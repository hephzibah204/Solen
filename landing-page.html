Common questions

Everything you need to know

Is Solen a replacement for therapy?
No — and we're clear about that. Solen is a personal wellness coaching companion, not a licensed therapist or medical service. Many users find it works well alongside therapy, or as a daily check-in tool between sessions. For clinical concerns, please see a professional.
How does the memory actually work?
After each session, Solen quietly extracts themes and emotional patterns from your conversation and stores them privately. In your next session, your coach references what you've shared before — so every conversation genuinely builds on the last. You can delete your data at any time.
What happens after my free trial?
After 7 days you'll choose a plan — Pro at $12.99/mo or Premium at $24.99/mo. If you don't upgrade, your account moves to read-only. Your data is never deleted. We also offer yearly plans that save up to 40%.
Is my data private?
Yes. Your conversations are encrypted and never sold or shared with third parties. You can export or delete everything from your account settings at any time.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — one tap from your account settings. If you're within 30 days of a paid charge and it didn't work for you, we'll refund it, no questions asked.
Does voice work on mobile?
Yes — Chrome and Safari on iOS and Android. You'll be prompted to allow mic access the first time. Voice mode is optimised for hands-free use, so it works well on walks, commutes, or before bed.
